Book a Table

Choose how you want
to spend the night.

Entry and regular live entertainment at Moonshine are free. Reserving a table gives you a guaranteed place, the option to choose the area that suits your plans and table service during your visit.

Areas you can book

Theatre Stage

Guaranteed seating with prime views of live performances in Moonshine's most intimate setting. The right choice when the artist is the reason for your visit.

Speakeasy Bar

Booths and relaxed seating for social drinks and larger groups. You will remain close to the atmosphere, although views of live performances are not guaranteed from every table.

Members Bar

A more private, refined setting with comfortable booth seating. Availability may be limited by private events and peak periods.

Sports Bar

Comfortable booths, large screens, a darts board, pool table and a fully stocked bar. Available for selected sports screenings and group bookings.

Reserve your
perfect spot.

Pick your date, party size and time. Table reservations carry a booking charge, which guarantees your seating and table service throughout your visit.

Not sure which area to choose? Tell us what brings you to Moonshine and the team will help where availability allows.

Before you book

  • Moonshine is strictly 18+ with a Challenge 25 policy. Bring photo ID.
  • Smart dress code. No hoodies, caps, sportswear or sports trainers.
  • Walk-ins are welcome, subject to capacity. On Friday and Saturday nights and for live performances, booking ahead is recommended.
  • For Bottomless Brunch, book through the brunch page.
