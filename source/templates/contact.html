Getting here

Moonshine is located on High Street in the heart of Leicester city centre.

By train
Leicester Railway Station is approximately a 15-minute walk from the venue, making us an easy destination whether you're visiting from across the city or further afield.
By car
The closest parking is available at Highcross Rooftop Car Park, just a short walk from Moonshine. Additional city centre parking is also available nearby.
By bus
St Margaret's Bus Station is approximately a 10-minute walk away, with regular services connecting Leicester and the surrounding areas.
Finding us
Our entrance is located on High Street. Look out for the Moonshine signage and step inside to discover Leicester's iconic speakeasy.
