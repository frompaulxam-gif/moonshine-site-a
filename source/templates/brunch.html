Bottomless Brunch

Bottomless Brunch,
the Moonshine way.

Step inside for ninety minutes of free-flowing drinks, curated grazing boards and live entertainment in one of Leicester's most atmospheric venues.

{{ m.btn('Book Bottomless Brunch', '#book', 'red') }}

Made for birthdays, catch-ups and afternoons that deserve to become evenings, Moonshine Bottomless Brunch is £45 per person and takes place on selected Saturdays.

What is included

  • 90 minutes of bottomless prosecco, draught beer and soft drinks.
  • Your own make-your-own bubbly cocktail kit.
  • A curated grazing board - choose mixed meat and cheese, cheese-only or vegan.
  • Live entertainment throughout your visit.
  • The option to upgrade to premium cocktails on the day.

Good to know

Please tell us about dietary requirements when booking so the team can prepare the right board for your table.

Your booking is for a timed 90-minute bottomless experience. Please arrive promptly so you can enjoy the full sitting. The venue's 18+ and ID policies apply.
