About Moonshine

A different kind of
night out in Leicester.

Moonshine is a speakeasy-inspired bar hidden in the heart of Leicester city centre - a place built around atmosphere, performance and the pleasure of staying for one more drink.

Inside, live artists perform close to the crowd. Vinyl DJs move between soul, jazz, funk, Motown, hip hop, reggae and late-night favourites. Cocktails arrive at candlelit tables. Different rooms create different ways to spend the evening, from the energy of the main space to the privacy of the Members Bar and the relaxed pace of the Sports Bar.

The programme changes, but the intention stays the same: to offer Leicester nights with character.

Not a museum.
Not an ordinary bar.

The influence of the speakeasy is there in the hidden setting, rich textures and sense of occasion. Moonshine is not about asking every guest to play a part. It is about giving people somewhere that feels removed from the everyday, while staying warm, welcoming and unmistakably part of modern Leicester.

The night is the headline

Live music, DJs, brunches, private celebrations and new event ideas all have a place here.
