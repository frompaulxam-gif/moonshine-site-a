If you have a public-facing event you want to bring to life, we would love to hear it. Music, spoken word, comedy, creative workshops, paint and sip evenings, community gatherings, launches and ideas that do not fit neatly into a category are all welcome.

Thursday evenings are particularly well suited to new concepts and collaborations, giving promoters, performers and organisers space to build something distinctive with the Moonshine team.

Ideas that belong here

What we welcome

  • Live music and acoustic sessions.
  • Open mic, spoken word and poetry.
  • Comedy, cabaret and performance.
  • Paint and sip evenings and creative workshops.
  • Listening parties and DJ concepts.
  • Book, product and creative launches.
  • Networking and community events.
  • Quizzes, screenings and participatory nights.
  • A strong idea we have not listed yet.

How collaboration works

Four steps

  1. Tell us the idea - the format, audience, preferred date and what makes the event special.
  2. We consider the fit - room, timing, capacity, technical needs and how Moonshine can support it.
  3. We shape the plan together - responsibilities, ticketing, promotion, production and hospitality.
  4. We open the doors - with a clear guest journey and a shared plan for the night.

What to include in your pitch

Share your idea

  • A concise description of the event.
  • Your preferred date or date range.
  • Expected audience size.
  • Previous examples, audience links or performer information, where relevant.
  • Technical, staging or furniture requirements.
  • Whether the event is free, ticketed or commercially sponsored.
  • What support you are seeking from Moonshine.

You do not need to arrive with every detail solved. Start with the idea, and we will tell you whether Moonshine can be the right home for it.
