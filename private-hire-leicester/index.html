Private Hire

Make the room
part of the story.

Some occasions deserve more than an ordinary function space. Hidden in Leicester city centre, Moonshine offers three distinctive areas for celebrations, company gatherings, launches, parties, photoshoots, video shoots and private events.

Enquire about private hire

From the atmosphere and drinks to entertainment and room layout, our team will help shape the setting around the occasion.

The spaces

Space 1 of 3

The Speakeasy Bar

Moonshine at its most recognisable - warm, intimate and made for evenings with atmosphere. The Speakeasy Bar is suited to celebrations, drinks receptions, live entertainment and events that deserve a distinctive backdrop.

Enquire about the Speakeasy Bar

Space 2 of 3

The Members Bar

A more private, refined room with comfortable booth seating and a tucked-away feel. The Members Bar works well for smaller celebrations, networking, team gatherings, hosted drinks and occasions that benefit from their own space.

Enquire about the Members Bar

Space 3 of 3

The Sports Bar

A relaxed space with a fully stocked bar, comfortable booths, large screens, a darts board and pool table. Ideal for sports screenings, informal company socials, group celebrations and private gatherings with a more casual pace.

Enquire about the Sports Bar
