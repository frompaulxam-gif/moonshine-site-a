The regulars

Every week at Moonshine

Moonshine Thursdays

Thursdays from 7pm · Free, ticket via Fatsoma

Our slower, more intimate midweek evenings, rotating between Trivia Night, Karaoke Night, Retro Movie Night and Open Mic Night, with the Nola Jam Sessions on selected third Thursdays. Events start from 7pm and are free to attend, just secure your ticket via Fatsoma. Afterwards our resident vinyl DJ keeps things moving with jazz, blues, soul and rock 'n' roll, and happy hour runs all night.

Secure your free ticket

Moonshine Fridays

Fridays, 6pm - 3am · Free entry

Live music on the Theatre Stage from 9.30pm, then resident DJ Benzo soundtracks the rest of the night with the biggest anthems from the 90s through to today. Happy hour 6pm to 8pm.

Book a table

Saturdays Live

Saturdays, 4pm - 3am · Free entry

Live music every Saturday from 9.30pm: jazz, blues, soul, Motown and rock 'n' roll, performed close enough to feel every note. Happy hour 5pm to 7pm.

Bottomless Brunch

Selected Saturdays from 4pm · £45 per person

Ninety minutes of bottomless prosecco, draught beer and soft drinks, a make-your-own bubbly cocktail kit, a curated grazing board and live entertainment. Booked and paid online.
