Open Mic Night brings live music, spoken word, poetry, storytelling and comedy to Moonshine. All talent levels are welcome and vintage vibes are encouraged. Performers sign up on the night or by email.

Moonshine Thursdays

Our slower, more intimate midweek evenings, rotating between Trivia Night, Karaoke Night, Retro Movie Night and Open Mic Night, with the Nola Jam Sessions on selected third Thursdays. Events start from 7pm and are free to attend, just secure your ticket via Fatsoma. Afterwards our resident vinyl DJ keeps things moving with jazz, blues, soul and rock 'n' roll, and happy hour runs all night.

Good to know

  • Moonshine is strictly 18+ with a Challenge 25 policy, so bring photo ID.
  • Entry is free but ticketed. Secure your place through Fatsoma before the night.
  • For the best view of the performance, book a table in the Theatre.
  • Smart dress code. No hoodies, caps, sportswear or sports trainers. Read the full dress code.
