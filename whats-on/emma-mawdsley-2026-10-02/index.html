Moonshine Fridays

Live music on the Theatre Stage from 9.30pm, then resident DJ Benzo soundtracks the rest of the night with the biggest anthems from the 90s through to today. Happy hour 6pm to 8pm.

Good to know

  • Moonshine is strictly 18+ with a Challenge 25 policy, so bring photo ID.
  • Entry and the live entertainment are free. Reserving a table guarantees your seat and adds table service.
  • For the best view of the performance, book a table in the Theatre.
  • Smart dress code. No hoodies, caps, sportswear or sports trainers. Read the full dress code.
